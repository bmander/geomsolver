# Solid sweeps, built exactly

A solid sweep is everything a tool passes through as a motion carries it: `sweep(tool over t in
(a, b), under: m)`, and most often `cut` from a blank, as a machine cuts. Any such sweep can be
read point by point (the material field), but its boundary is built exactly — written by
`solventc --step`/`--stl` and shown as a smooth surface — only for the classes a tool and a motion
fall into whose boundary has a known structure, each admitted by its own rows and refused by name
outside them ([docs/generating-sweeps.md](../../../docs/generating-sweeps.md)). The classes grow
one at a time (#684, #721); each has its simplest case here, and every file's volume is a figure its
comments give.

Read them in this order:

| File | Class | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `ball.sv` | the canal | **A ball's sweep is its tube.** A ball swung a quarter turn about an upright axis, half sunk in a block: the tube about its centre's path with half a ball at each end, whatever the motion turns it by. Pappus. |
| `path.sv` | the canal, along any path | **A ball carried along any curve.** A ball riding a frame along a spline in a block's top face: the tube about its centre's path, fitted to it, with half a ball at each end. The volume is π r² L + 4/3 π r³ with L the spline's length. |
| `slide.sv` | constant twist: a slide | **A tool slid along a line.** A torus slid up a rod, clear at both ends: the wall is the wheel's characteristic extruded, here its tube's circle, and the groove the lens the rod and the tube share. Tilt it and the wall is no circle of it. |
| `turn.sv` | constant twist: a turn | **A tool turned partway about an axis.** A torus leaning across the turn swung over a curved block: the wall is its characteristic laid in a half-plane through the axis and revolved. Pappus over the torus's shadow. |
| `rocked.sv` | constant twist: a turn, driven by a law | **A tool rocked out and back by a law.** `turn.sv`'s cutter swung from one end of its turn to the other and back, slowing to a stop at each, as a cam drives a rocker: a sweep is its path's, not its pace's, so a joint driven by a law sweeps what the joint does over the least to the greatest value the law reaches, and the groove is `turn.sv`'s, built as a turn's. |
| `whole_turn.sv` | constant twist: a whole turn | **A tool turned a whole turn.** A V wheel leaning across a rod turned once round it, as a rod turns in a lathe against a form tool, starting and ending inside it: a sweep is its path's, so the groove is a solid of revolution, its wall the wheel's characteristic (each flank's, joined round the rim) revolved all the way round and closing on itself. Pappus over the wheel's shadow. |
| `screw.sv` | constant twist: a screw | **A tool carried along a helix.** The same wheel screwed up the rod: the characteristic is read once on the wheel and carried along the helix, so the groove's section is the same at every height, turned. |
| `vthread.sv` | constant twist: a screw with sharp edges | **A tool with a sharp edge carried along a helix.** A ring with a 60° V rim screwed up the rod, as a thread is ground: each flank's characteristic meets the rim at its own point, and the wall runs between them round the rim, where every normal between the flanks is the wheel's. The groove's section, the same at every height, is read point by point from the V. |
| `square_thread.sv` | constant twist: a prism | **A flat-sided tool carried along a helix.** A rectangular bar standing across a rod, screwed along it as a lathe cuts a square thread: the screw moves each ruling of the bar as one affine field, so the bar touches at one point a ruling, along its tip's middle and out along the corners where it meets its flanks. The thread's root is the tip's cylinder, and the groove widens outward, by the turn a corner point makes to come round to the tip's plane. No roll is stated: the screw carries the bar through the rod however long it is. In closed form. |
| `flat_end.sv` | constant twist: a prism's cap in the blank | **A tool that cuts with its end, turned.** A bar leaning out from upright, its flat end facing down toward the axis, turned across a curved block: the end touches along the line where the plane through the axis and its normal meets it, which the turn carries round into the cut's floor, a cone; the inner side touches along a line crossing its rulings, the wall; and the rim between them carries the contact from the one to the other round its fan. The block loses what the bar's shadow covers, read slice by slice. |
| `creased.sv` | constant twist: a prism drafted both ways | **A tool drafted from a parting line, turned.** A bar leaning across the turn, drafted 2° either side of the plane its section is drawn in: each side folds there in a convex crease. Each half of its inner side touches along its own line, a little either side of the plane through the axis, and the crease's fan carries the contact from the one to the other. The block loses what the bar's shadow covers, read slice by slice. |
| `facing.sv` | constant twist: a prism's ruling in contact all along it | **A tool that touches along a whole line, turned.** A bar leaning out from upright in a plane through the axis, its inner side facing the axis squarely, standing past a curved block above and below: each ruling of that side touches all along it or nowhere, and the side touches along the one in the plane through the axis, which the turn carries round into the wall, a cone. In closed form. |
| `slot.sv` | planar subtraction: an end mill slid | **A tool that cuts with its flat end, slid.** An end mill standing upright slid across a block: read from its B-rep, the mill is a cylinder and two flat ends standing along its axis, which is the normal of the planes the slide keeps, so the class reads its section, a disc, and the region the disc sweeps, a stadium, carried down through the slab its flat end bounds: the slot, its walls the disc's contacts and its floor the flat end's plane. In closed form. |
| `vgroove.sv` | constant twist: a sharp edge along one point's path | **A tool whose sharp rim meets the motion edge on, slid.** A V wheel lying across a block slid along it square to its axis: each flank touches along its meridian square to the slide, and the two meet the rim at one point, where the rim runs along the slide and every normal between the flanks is the wheel's. The walls are the flanks' meridians slid, two planes meeting in a sharp edge along that point's path, the groove's root. In closed form. |
| `punch.sv` | planar subtraction: a tool passed wholly through | **A tool passed wholly through, slid.** A drum lying on its side pushed edge first up through a plate: read from its B-rep, it stands along its axis, square to which the push keeps every plane, so its section, a rectangle, sweeps the slot's section, and the region is carried through the drum's length: the plate loses the drum's shadow through its thickness. In closed form. |
| `drill.sv` | constant twist: a ring in contact all round | **A tool slid along its own axis.** A drill with a 118° point pushed down through a plate: each ring of the drill meets the slide at one angle all round, so it touches nowhere or all round, and the ring of its greatest radius sweeps the cylinder of that radius, the hole's wall, written exactly. The drill passes wholly through and its shadow along the slide is its whole disc, so the plate loses that cylinder through its thickness, whatever the point. In closed form. |
| `spoke.sv` | constant twist: a ring in contact all round, turned | **A tool whose axis meets the turn's.** A pin lying along a radius of the turn swung across a curved block: every ring of it touches at its two points in the plane of the two axes, its side along its top and bottom lines, the slot's roof and floor, and the ring of its end's rim whose normals pass through the centre touches all round, sweeping a zone of the sphere about the centre, the slot's end wall. The branch beyond that ring, across the pin's end, runs within the zone and is trimmed. In closed form. |
| `plunge.sv` | the closed class: a slide | **A tool plunged partway, aslant.** A bar of square section pushed up a slanted line into a plate, stopping halfway through: no contact walk reads it, its silhouette turning at the bar's vertices and its end in the plate. The kernel splits the plate at once by every face the boundary can lie on — the bar's faces where the push enters and leaves them, its silhouette edges swept — and keeps each cell the bar, carried back, misses (#721). The plate loses each section across the bar swept along the push and clipped to it. |
| `stopped_punch.sv` | the closed class: a curved tool slid | **A round tool plunged partway, aslant.** `punch.sv`'s drum pushed up a slanted line into the plate, stopping halfway through: no contact walk reads it, its flat ends leaning to the push and its end in the plate. The plate is split at once by the drum where the push enters and leaves it, its side's two lines square to the push swept into the planes tangent along them, and each rim swept into an oblique cylinder, and each cell the drum, carried back, misses is kept (#721). The plate loses each section across the drum swept along the push and clipped to it. |
| `stopped_drum.sv` | the closed class: a curved tool turned | **A round tool turned partway, stopping inside.** A short drum standing about an axis along the turn's, swung from clear of a ring sector to halfway across it: its side touches along its two lines in the plane through both axes, which turn into cylinders about the axis, and where it stops is its round side at the end pose, among the candidates the ring is split by at once. Each circle about the axis loses the angle the drum covers from the sector's edge to where it stops. |
| `stopped_bullnose.sv` | the closed class: a torus turned | **A rounded tool turned partway, stopping inside.** `stopped_drum.sv`'s drum with its bottom corner rounded, a bull-nose mill, swung from clear of the ring sector to halfway across it. The round is a torus about the mill's axis, along the turn's: it touches round its meridians in the plane through both axes, turned into tori about the turn's axis, and round its bottom ring, kept in its plane, and at each end pose the round touches each meridian's torus round that meridian, met in closed form. Each circle about the axis loses the angle each level of the mill covers from the sector's edge to where it stops. |
| `leaning_ring.sv` | the closed class: a ring slid at a lean | **A ring pushed into a block at a lean, stopping inside.** A torus whose axis leans 35° from upright pushed straight down until wholly in a block: it touches along two closed curves, the outer and the inner half of its tube, each smooth where a level's two points of it meet at its top and bottom, and each carried down into the cylinder over its outline seen along the push — the hole's wall and the wall of the core the ring's own hole leaves standing. The block is split at once by the ring where it stops and those two cylinders, met in closed form. Every line down the push meets the ring once or not at all, so the block loses the centre's depth times the annulus between the outlines and half the ring, `π² R r²`. Leaning past `tan² β = (R − r)/r` the inner outline would cusp, and that is refused by name. |
| `leaning_bullnose.sv` | the closed class: a bull-nose's round slid at a lean | **A bull-nose mill pushed into a block at a lean, stopping inside.** A mill whose bottom corner is a quarter round, its axis leaning 30° from upright, pushed straight down until wholly in a block: its round touches along the half of its outer contact that lies on it, ending where the contact crosses the seam into the side's two lines whose normal is square to the push. Carried down, the round's contact is the sheet over its outline's arc and each of those lines the plane it is tangent along, and the two touch along the path of the point where the contact crosses the seam — a touch the kernel is given by construction, not left to find. Every line down the push meets the mill once, so the block loses each line's depth to the mill's underside: the flux of `(0, 0, z)` out of the faces turned down. A round meeting its neighbour in a sharp rim at a lean is refused by name. |
| `spline_mill.sv` | the closed class: any rigid motion | **A bull-nose mill riding a frame along a spline.** `path.sv`'s spline, a bull-nose leaning 30° toward where it goes, its round sunk in a block: the frame turns the mill as the spline turns, so no constant twist carries it, and its contact — down its side, round its round, up its side and over its top rim — moves on it from instant to instant. The kernel reads that curve at each instant in a pencil of half-planes about the mill's velocity, fits the sheet it sweeps over the roll, and splits the block at once by it and by the mill where it starts and where it stops, their touches given. Each point of the groove is entered once and left once, so the block loses what the mill holds below its top where it starts and the rate its faces below the top advance outward, `∫ ∫ (n · v)⁺ dA dt`. |
| `helical_groove.sv` | the closed class: a curved tool screwed | **A round tool screwed: helical milling.** `stopped_drum.sv`'s drum swung wholly across the ring sector while it rises half a millimetre a turn, as an end mill mills a helical groove: its flat ends keep no plane, so no contact walk closes it. The ring is split at once by the drum at both end poses, its side's two lines in the plane through both axes screwed into cylinders about the axis, and each rim's disc screwed into the thin ramp it sweeps, met in closed form; each cell the drum, screwed back, misses is kept. The ring loses its own height and the rise over the drum's width, `π/3 (20 + k π)`. |
| `ball_end_mill.sv` | the closed class: a ball and its side screwed | **A ball-end mill screwed: helical milling with a round end.** An end mill whose end is a half ball swung wholly across the ring sector while it rises 3 mm a turn. The ball sweeps the pipe about its centre's helix, which touches the cylinder the side screws into along the helix of the ball's outer equator; the ring is split at once by the mill at both end poses, the side's two lines screwed into cylinders about the axis and the ball's great circle square to its centre's velocity screwed into the pipe, and each cell the mill, screwed back, misses is kept. In helical coordinates a point is removed where its helix meets the mill, and the ring loses `π/3 (40 + ∫ ρ M dρ)`, `M` how far a helix reaches across the ball. |
| `leaning_mill.sv` | the closed class: sharp circles leaning to a turn | **A flat-end mill leaning across a turn, its corners digging past its ends.** A drum with square ends leaning 25° from upright, beside the plane through the axis, swung 100° inside a ring. Its side touches along two curves, each turned into a surface of revolution held exactly; its flat ends' contact lines miss them, and its rims' sharp corners carry the cut past them: each rim turns into the ring its circle sweeps, whose meridian is an ellipse in `(ρ² + z², z)`. At each end pose the end's plane and the side meet that ring along the rim in closed form. Each level of the mill is convex and clear of the axis, so the ring loses the mill and `100/360` of the volume it sweeps turned whole. |
| `stopped_turn.sv` | the closed class: a turn | **A tool turned partway, stopping inside.** A small bar swung about the axis from clear of a ring sector to halfway across it: where it stops is its own faces at the end pose, among the candidates the ring is split by at once, with its edges and its sides' contact lines turned. Each circle about the axis loses the angle the bar covers from the sector's edge to where it stops. |
| `stopped_screw.sv` | the closed class: a screw | **A thread stopped short.** `square_thread.sv`'s bar screwed only halfway up the rod, as a thread stops short of a shoulder: the rod is split at once by the bar's faces at both end poses, its edges and sides' contact lines screwed into cylinders and its level edges into helicoidal sheets, met in closed form. Each circle of the rod loses the angle the bar covers over the rolls that reach its height. |
| `rack.sv` | generating | **A tool rolled on a turning blank.** A rack tooth slid along the pitch line while the blank turns: one tooth space, its flanks involutes nobody draws. The tool's contact moves over it as it rolls, so each point of the wall is a contact at its own instant. |
| `helical.sv` | generating, with a drafted prism | **A drafted tool leaned across the blank.** `rack.sv`'s tooth drawn in a plane leaned 15° across the blank, as a helical rack's teeth are, and its sides drafted 2°, so it thins as it goes deeper. The motion keeps no plane of the tool, and each drafted side is a plane whose contact is a line across it at each instant. Each section of the space square to the blank's axis is the tooth's section there, a quadrilateral, swept as `rack.sv`'s tooth is. |
| `parted.sv` | generating, with a prism drafted both ways | **A tool drafted from a parting line.** `helical.sv`'s tooth with its plane moved to the middle of the blank and drafted 2° either side of it, as a tool forged or cast from a parting line is: thickest where it crosses the blank's middle, each side folding there in a convex crease. Every normal between a side's two halves' is the tooth's at the crease, so its fan carries the contact from the one half's envelope to the other's, as a sharp edge's does. Each section of the space square to the blank's axis is the tooth's section there, its eight half-planes meeting in it. |
| `bar_end.sv` | generating: a prism's cap in the blank | **A tool that cuts with its end.** A tapered bar pointing at a disc's centre, rolled as a rack's tooth: its flat end keeps 18 mm from the centre, so the end face cuts the space's floor and the rims round it carry its walls, while its sides never touch. The bar is sectioned across its length, each section a chord of its profile carried along it, so its walk runs from a side over the end's rim and across the end. Each height of the disc loses the space its own slice of the bar sweeps. |
| `next_tooth.sv` | generating: a placed prism's cap in the blank | **A tool drawn once and placed.** `bar_end.sv`'s bar placed a quarter turn of the roll along the rack, as a rack's further teeth are, and rolled a quarter turn earlier: the placement carries the bar's sections across, and its end cuts the same space turned a quarter turn round the disc. |
| `conjugate.sv` | planar subtraction | **A tool always in mesh.** A plus turning against a wheel over a whole period of the pair: never clear of the blank, so the region its section sweeps in the plane is read exactly and the wheel is the blank less its prism. |
| `union.sv` | constant twist, a union swept whole | **A tool that is several tools.** A wheel of two beads that overlap, slid up a rod: where they meet the wheel's profile turns inward, a concave corner where the slide's characteristic ends on each bead and the sheets are trimmed against each other, so the union is swept as the one solid it is (a union of separate solids, or under a turn seen from a turn, is still its members' sweeps). Each bead's shadow is its tube's circle; the groove is what of the rod either covers. |

Larger cases of each class: the spiral bevel and hypoid pair (`spiral_bevel/`, generating), the
rack-cut spur gear (`generation/rack_cut_gear.sv`), the twist drill and the straight-fluted drill
(`twist_drill/`, a screw and a slide), the Wankel rotor (`wankel/`, the planar generating class,
whose pocket's inner envelope has no smaller case) and the swept spring (`swept_spring.sv`, a ball
on a screw).

A sweep the other classes refuse, of a tool whose every face is a plane and every edge a line,
under one slide, turn or screw (under a screw, its faces and edges along the axis or square to it),
or under a slide a tool whose faces are planes, cylinders, cones and balls (and tori: a whole
ring at any lean, or a round's band meeting its neighbours smoothly) and whose edges are lines and circles, under a turn one whose round faces (cylinders, cones, tori) stand about axes along the turn's, a cylinder or a cone about any axis, and sharp circles however they lean, or under a screw a drum or a frustum about an axis along the screw's, or a ball, alone or under such a side (a ball-end mill), or under any other rigid motion — a frame along a path — a convex tool of revolution (a ball, a drum, a bull-nose), is the closed class's: cut from its blank on one arrangement, whether it stops inside the
blank, undercuts or meets itself (`plunge.sv`, `stopped_punch.sv`, `leaning_ring.sv`, `leaning_bullnose.sv`, `stopped_drum.sv`, `stopped_bullnose.sv`, `helical_groove.sv`, `ball_end_mill.sv`, `leaning_mill.sv`, `spline_mill.sv`, `stopped_turn.sv`,
`stopped_screw.sv`). Outside every class — a tumbling tool (`swept_tumble.sv`), a
a sharp circle leaning to a screw's axis — the sweep is read by its field and drawn by the page, and its export is refused,
saying which row it fails.

`rust/gcs-core/tests/solid_sweeps_examples.rs` holds each file to its class, to an exact build
whose STL agrees with the field, and to its volume; a file added here is named in this README and
read by a test there, or that file's last test fails.
